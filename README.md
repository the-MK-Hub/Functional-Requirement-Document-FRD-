# 🏭 Change Management System – FRD Analysis
This repository contains my analysis and structured understanding of a Functional Requirement Document (FRD) provided as part of a recruitment assignment.

## 📌 Objective
- To interpret business requirements and translate them into system-level understanding  
- To demonstrate clarity on workflows, roles, and key functional components  
- To highlight the difference between Business Analysis and Data Analysis  

## 📄 Overview of the System
The system is a Change Management module used in regulated environments to:
- Manage change requests  
- Track approvals and workflows  
- Perform risk and impact assessments  
- Maintain audit trails for compliance  

## 🧩 Key Functional Areas Identified

### 1. Change Request Initiation
- Users can create and submit change requests  
- Includes details like description, reason, and urgency  

### 2. Workflow Management
- Defined lifecycle:
  Draft → Review → Impact Assessment → Approval → Implementation → Closure  
- Role-based flow involving Initiator, Reviewer, Approver, and QA  

### 3. Impact Assessment
- Mandatory evaluation by multiple departments  
- Ensures quality and operational impact is assessed  

### 4. Risk Assessment
- Uses impact and probability to determine risk level  
- Based on standard risk management principles  

### 5. Electronic Signatures
- Requires authentication and reason for approval  
- Ensures secure and accountable approvals  

### 6. Audit Trail
- Tracks all actions with timestamp and user details  
- Ensures traceability and accountability  

### 7. Notifications
- Alerts for pending approvals and deadlines  

## 🔐 Compliance Understanding
- Follows data integrity principles (ALCOA+)  
- Supports validation processes (IQ, OQ, PQ)  

## 🧠 Key Learnings
- Understood how business requirements are converted into system workflows  
- Learned the importance of compliance in regulated systems  
- Identified how roles and approvals are structured in enterprise applications  
- Differentiated between:
  - Business Analyst → focuses on requirements, workflows, and system design  
  - Data Analyst → focuses on data insights and reporting  

## 📌 Conclusion
This assignment helped in understanding how real-world business requirements are structured and how a Business Analyst translates them into functional system components.
